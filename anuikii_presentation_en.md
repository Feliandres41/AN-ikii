# ANüikii

### Presentation Script

**Learning support application | SENA Regional La Guajira**

---

> **Opening**  
> Good morning. Today, I would like to introduce **ANüikii**, a mobile application created to support the education and learning processes of the SENA community, particularly at SENA Regional La Guajira.

## 01 · Why was it created?

ANüikii was created to make it easier to learn and look up **technical terms and English vocabulary**. The app brings this content closer to everyday life through digital tools such as a glossary and a visual recognition feature.

## 02 · General objective

> To support SENA learners through a digital experience that makes it easier to look up technical vocabulary, practice English words, and access institutional information.

## 03 · What does the app do?

ANüikii brings together tools for looking up and practicing vocabulary:

- **Technical glossary:** presents terms in Spanish alongside their English equivalents.
- **Visual recognition:** uses the camera to identify an object and display its name in both languages.
- **Pronunciation support:** provides a pronunciation guide and lets users hear the English term.
- **Usage examples:** presents sentences in the past, present, and future tenses.
- **Search history:** lets users review previously searched terms.
- **Institutional features:** includes sign-in, user profile information, and rankings related to programs, training cohorts, and apprentices.
- **Personal settings:** offers options such as choosing the visual theme.

## 04 · What makes it different?

ANüikii combines resources that are often found separately: **a technical glossary, camera-based object recognition, and English practice with pronunciation and examples**. Users can start with an object in front of them to learn its name in English and how to use the word.

The app is also designed for the SENA institutional context and includes information and features related to its programs and training cohorts. Its distinctive value lies in bringing practical vocabulary learning and institutional tools together in one app.

## 05 · Who is it for?

It is primarily intended for **apprentices at SENA Regional La Guajira** who want to look up technical terms and strengthen their English vocabulary. It may also be useful to other members of the SENA educational community, depending on their access and the features enabled for their profile.

## Closing

In conclusion, ANüikii aims to make learning technical vocabulary and English more practical by combining lookups, camera-based learning, pronunciation, and usage examples. **The current version is at an early stage and requires institutional validation before broader deployment.**

*End of presentation*
